Bandit Territory

by Paul Bishop

Disorderly conduct opens the door to darker crimes in ten new stories of murder and mayhem from Paul Bishop, O'Neil De Noux, Wayne D. Dundee, Brian Drake, Mike A. Baron, James Hopwood, Bill Craig, Bobby Nash, Jean Rabe, and Nicholas Cain.

Words from Paul Bishop

In this sequel to Criminal Tendencies, things turn bad at every turn. Bandit territory is a place you don't go unless you're alert, armed, and carrying backup. It appears in boardrooms, political campaigns, and high-stakes poker rooms—places where the rules don't apply, the knives come out, and fortunes and lives get destroyed in a heartbeat. But the most dangerous bandit territory is the mind, which is where crime and mystery writers thrive. Enter bandit territory at your own risk...

Race the Peril

World Gone Dark 2

by January Bain

The world has gone lethal, and the Mackenzie clan's fragile alliances are breaking under arrests, earthquakes, armed threats, and impossible choices. In Hardin Creek, trusting the wrong person could mean no one sees morning.

Words from January Bain

People often ask me why I choose to spend my days in a world that has ended. When the lights go out and the noise of the modern world finally stops, the only currency that has ever had real value remains—each other. Writing the Mackenzies has been a journey of discovery for me. I write because I believe that even when the air becomes a weapon and the mountains fall, the human heart remains a sanctuary.
