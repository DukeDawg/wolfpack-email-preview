Closest Thing to a Doctor (Bonner and Claudia Adventure 1)

by C.K. Crigger

Hotel worker Claudia Kennedy saves recovery agent Bonner Galloway after a search for a stolen Appaloosa nearly gets him killed. Then the hotel owner is murdered, and Claudia becomes the prime suspect. With corrupt lawmen closing in, the pair must uncover the truth while an unexpected attraction takes hold.

Words from C.K. Crigger

I’m always excited when I start a new series, and I’m delighted with this one. CLOSEST THING TO A DOCTOR releases October 27, 2026, and I have my fingers crossed that readers will enjoy it as much as I enjoyed writing it. Two Bonner and Claudia Adventures are written, and I’m well into the third. I think I’ve fallen a little in love with Bonner Galloway—and I trust Claudia Kennedy is his perfect match, both in romance and adventure.

Set around 1888, the story gives Bonner an unconventional job: recovery agent. He finds items that have been stolen, lost, or simply disappeared. Sometimes he has to be downright devious. People, he’s found, don’t always want those missing things recovered.

Claudia learned everything she knows by following her doctor father around from childhood. After his death, she never got to attend the medical school she’d counted on. Now she’s the closest thing to a doctor in their small, remote town in eastern Washington Territory. Not that she ever claims to be a doctor. And she doesn’t charge. What she does is probably illegal!

You might be surprised by how much research a story like this requires—especially the doctor parts. Personally, I’m a little (a lot) squeamish about blood.

So their story begins. Next up is THE RIGHT MAN FOR THE JOB. Stay tuned…

Enjoy! C.K.
