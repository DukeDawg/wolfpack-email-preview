Khan (Biker 8)

by Mike Baron

A missing Siberian tiger and a vanished former MMA fighter send Josh Pratt into a dangerous hunt. Hired to bring the animal back alive, he follows their trail through a dangerous criminal underworld, tangling with violent relatives, gangsters, and a sanctuary owner whose cruelty reaches far beyond the cages.

Words from Mike Baron

Josh Pratt belonged to the Bedouins. They were the only family he ever knew. Young and wild, he went to prison, found God, and got out determined to turn his life around. He’s not a wisecracker. He’s soft-spoken and reticent; the longest speech he ever gave was about the modifications to his Harley Road King.

He’s rescued dogs and people, found a famous rocker believed dead, and tracked killers from Wisconsin to Hollywood. But he’s never been asked to find a missing tiger.

Former MMA fighter Gena Kropenski made a life-changing mistake when she fell for Fabian Fitch, the slick hustler behind a notorious Wisconsin tiger sanctuary. When the state shuts down the operation for its inhumane treatment of the animals, Josh is hired to provide security while the sick and abused cats are moved to safety.

Then the sanctuary’s star attraction—a seven-hundred-pound Siberian tiger named Genghis Khan—vanishes. So does Gena. Hired to bring Khan back alive, Josh follows their trail, battling Gena’s violent brother, Fitch’s criminal associates, and gangster Tony Capadona.

Inspired by John D. MacDonald’s Travis McGee series, Biker is modern noir. When Josh discovers where Gena is headed, the search becomes a race against time. Her reason for taking Khan—and her ingenious method—may surprise you.

Some beasts belong in the wild. Others must be hunted down.

Mike Baron
