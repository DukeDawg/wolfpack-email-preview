Chaos Theory: A Western Double (Ariel's Profit 5)

Two adventures carry Ariel, Profit, and their companions from medieval Japan to territorial Arizona. Protecting young Yeshua means facing a relentless enemy across time, while a mission involving two immortal Rangers challenges everything they believe about destiny, redemption, and death.

Words from JJ Bonham

When books or movies get burned or banned, it’s another step toward totalitarianism. Boy, I’d hate to get that word in a spelling bee.

So, exorcise whatever is keeping you from reading Ariel’s Profit and get going—into the past, the future, and wherever you have a God-given right to be.

Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer may have been onto something when they placed the Latin motto beside their roaring lion: Ars gratia artis—art for art’s sake. To me, that means art doesn’t have to serve a moral, political, or useful purpose. Now, that’s freedom.

Your freedoms aren’t free, as is often said. And they may not be around forever. So read a time-travel novel and stick it to the Commies!

JJ Bonham
