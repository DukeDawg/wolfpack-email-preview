Cherokee Cry (The Long Hunter’s Journals 2)

by B.N. Rundell

Raised near the Cherokee town of Chota, Colton Frazier dreams of following his father into the Appalachian frontier. With a deep respect for the wilderness and its people, the young longhunter sets out for Kentucke—and discovers how fiercely the backcountry will test him.

Words from B.N. Rundell

Cherokee Cry—the second book in my new series, the Longhunter’s Journals. And like my characters, Kentucky has been a new adventure for us. We recently moved here from almost a lifetime in the Rocky Mountains of Colorado, and it has been a learning experience!

I prefer to write about the time in history that saw virgin country explored by intrepid adventurers—and for Kentucky, that time was about a hundred years before the west of the Rockies! For me, it meant learning about new natives, new explorers, different terrain and climate—whew! My brain was beginning to smoke due to overload!

But I like the new series—set a little different, with different main characters that don’t even meet until late in the stories—new places, new friends, and more! Join me, won’t you?

B.N. Rundell
