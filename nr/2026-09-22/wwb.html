Texas Promise: Hope and Opportunity (The Wolf’s Tales 6)

by Mark Greathouse

Isa O’Toole builds a Texas cattle ranch on faith, then faces raiders, ruthless cattle barons, and a rustling scheme that threatens his future. From Texas to Wyoming, he and his family must hold fast to courage and purpose in this young adult Western adventure.

Words from Mark Greathouse

Famed philosopher Aldous Huxley once said, “The secret of genius is to carry the spirit of the child into old age, which means never losing your enthusiasm.” As I’ve aged, that pretty much expresses the enthusiasm with which I approach my authoring. So it is that I call upon that enthusiasm in writing my sixth book in the Wolf’s Tales series, Texas Promise: Hope and Opportunity.

Hope and opportunity drive my life. Thus, it is with that mantra that Isa’s adventures call upon courage, faith, endurance, and pure grit as he builds a life with purpose on the American frontier. Through triumph and tragedy, Isa forges friendships and deals with enemies, as he learns to deal with his being half White and half Comanche. He finds his faith tested by the land and people. He and his Lakota wife Awentia continue to build their ranches in Wyoming and Texas, as they forge a life of purpose grounded in hope, opportunity, and faith.

As I sit in local coffee shops authoring Western tales, curious folks often pause to ask me what I’m doing. A common denominator as I meet fellow authors is an immersive passion for creating stories that deliver messages they’re committed to. We authors seek to draw readers into our stories, sharing the enthusiastically delivered passions embedded in our creations.

Just as my character Isa O’Toole is on a lifelong vision quest, I seek to draw upon my “spirit of the child” as I passionately deliver on a message of hope, opportunity, and faith.

Mark Greathouse
