Scott Stiletto: The Complete Series

by Brian Drake

Nine espionage thrillers send veteran CIA operator Scott Stiletto into a world of terrorists, traitors, assassins, and criminal empires. From covert extractions to deeply personal rescue missions, he must rely on his guns and wits as betrayal closes in and every assignment demands a greater sacrifice.

Words from Brian Drake

I’ve either come full circle or I’m circling the drain—not sure which—but this Stiletto box set sure brings back memories. I dreamed up the character in my eighth-grade math class when I should have been learning algebra (your X isn’t coming back, stop asking). All these years later, it’s not only gratifying to see the books in print, but it’s also nice to get questions from readers about when he’s coming back.

Sadly, I’m not sure Stiletto is returning, though I have a banger of a tenth book in mind should circumstances ever change. A lot of behind-the-scenes things still need to happen before he appears again (it might help if you send the publisher a note asking for more, too), and we’re not there yet. In the meantime, we have this great box set of all nine books.

Stiletto is like my first child—the one you make all the mistakes with and overworry about, but by the fourth kid you know what to do. I wish I could redo the books—I mean from scratch, seriously. Many of the story choices I made were cool in the moment, but I never thought about the directions they’d force me to follow later, and some of those choices hurt more than helped. Still, I’m the only one complaining. These books opened a ton of doors and brought me wonderful readers like you who’ve kept up with my work and it’s appreciated, believe me.

If this is your first time through the books, welcome—please stick around; we have plenty of good stuff on offer. If you’re revisiting, welcome back. We’re all a little older now and possibly jaded about many things, but at least we still know a good story when we find one.

Brian Drake
