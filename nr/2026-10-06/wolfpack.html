Jedediah Parks: The California Reckoning (Parks Brothers 2)

by Patrick Lindsay

After a brutal attack destroys his mule business, Jedediah Parks heads for California’s gold fields in search of a fresh start. With loyal friends beside him and a nurse he cannot forget, Jed faces outlaws, old enemies, and a patient hunter bent on revenge. Building a new life will mean fighting to keep it.

Words from Patrick Lindsay

The story of California in the days during and after the gold rush has always fascinated me. People came from all walks of life, driven by the opportunity provided by the gold strike.

Jedediah Parks, along with his twin brother Will, was one of those. His initial efforts in Oregon didn’t work out, but that didn’t stop him. He kept going, driven by his desire to succeed and by the opportunities this land gave him.

To me, it is a small slice of the story of America. I hope you enjoy reading Jedediah Parks as much as I enjoyed writing it.

Patrick Lindsay

The Tumbleweed Sagas: The Complete Series

by Mark Greathouse

Fourteen Western adventures follow two generations of Texas Rangers as Lucas “Long Luke” Dunn and his son Junior fight for justice and the families they love. From the Nueces Strip to a changing Texas shaped by railroads and oil, the Dunns face rustlers, vigilantes, criminal syndicates, and the enduring cost of wearing the badge.

Words from Mark Greathouse

The Tumbleweed Sagas are my tribute to the Texas Rangers. Inspired by my grandfather and several cousins who served as Texas Rangers and coupled with my love of the West, this series has grown to an epic tale forging a life in a rough and tumble world amidst the mingling aromas of gun smoke, leather, trail dust, perfumes, and bluebonnets. Mud in the streets, sawdust on saloon floors, bawling of longhorns, and the musky odor of crude oil titillate the senses.

Danger? Just about anywhere a Texas Ranger rides, death could be reaching for his reins. There’s an old Ranger saying, “One riot, one Ranger.” A Texas Ranger was said to be so tough that he’d take on a nest of rattlesnakes and spot them the first bite.

The characters and the story in the Tumbleweed Sagas series are fictional, but their personifications existed even as I’ve worked in actual historical figures of the time. Real events are juxtaposed against a backdrop of rugged but wildly beautiful landscapes. Luke Dunn’s life, and later his son Junior’s, is caught up with law and order on the rough and tumble hills and prairies of Texas while struggling with fundamental moral conflicts and obligations to wives and families.

This reveals an inherent contradiction in terms associated with the western genre, not the least of which is its malleability as it’s transitioned from gun fights and good versus evil to more complicated stories. While blending in personas of my Texas Ranger ancestors, I’ve crafted my Tumbleweed Sagas protagonist Luke Dunn and his son Junior as the prototypical Texas Rangers delivering justice on the Texas frontier. Saddle up and enjoy the read.

Mark Greathouse
