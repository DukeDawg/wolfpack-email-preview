Mail-Order Deception (The Brides of Evergreen 3)

by Heather Blanton

Reporter Ellie Blair poses as a mail-order bride to chase a career-making story in Wyoming. Her supposed rancher groom, Jim West, is an undercover Treasury agent closing in on the Irish Mob. With two false identities and an unexpected attraction between them, trusting each other may be their most dangerous assignment.

Words from Heather Blanton

I’ve always admired Nellie Bly, the iconic, ambitious female reporter. The nerve that woman had! She chased stories other people wouldn’t touch and inspired countless women to believe they could do the same. That’s where the character of Ellie Blair began for me: an ambitious reporter with something to prove and precious little concern for what trouble proving it might cause.

But I also wanted to write a Western with real teeth. The danger in this story isn’t window dressing, and the villain isn’t someone you’d want to meet on a deserted street—or a crowded one.

Which brings me to our hero, Jim West. If you’ve seen A Knight’s Tale, think “the protector of Italian virginity.” That kind of noble, heroic man…paired with a woman who doesn’t think she needs protecting.

She’ll risk almost anything for the truth. He’ll risk himself for her.

Writing those two at cross-purposes—with secrets, danger, and attraction complicating everything—was irresistible. I hope you enjoy it!

Heather Blanton
