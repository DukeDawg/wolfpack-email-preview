Blood on the Saddle (Texas Ranger Rayland Gibbs 3)

by Ash Lingam

With Laredo’s sheriff fighting for his life and a deputy dead, Ranger Sergeant Rayland Gibbs takes temporary command of the town’s law. His captain is away confronting chaos in El Paso, leaving Rayland short on men as he hunts the ambushers. One determined Ranger must hold the line before Laredo descends into bloodshed.

Words from Ash Lingam

When I began the first book in my new Texas Ranger series, Rayland Gibbs was already clear in my mind. By the time I finally sat down to write Dead Man Walking, the story seemed to unfold on its own, flowing freely and almost effortlessly. Now, in the third Rayland Gibbs adventure, I know these characters well enough to take them—and the reader—deeper into their world. As the series grows, new, compelling figures join the story.

Its two central men are like two sides of the same coin, despite their age difference. Rayland Gibbs is a young, rising Texas Ranger sergeant; Captain Lynch Moon is his commanding officer, a veteran hardened by years of pursuing outlaws and rustlers along the Rio Grande. Moon believes trouble follows Gibbs like a shadow, yet he also knows the post needs a man who can handle himself with a gun. Rayland proves it by shooting seven men in seven minutes. Some claim he is Texas’s fastest draw and finest shot.

In Blood on the Saddle, the sheriff of Laredo is shot, and his deputy is killed. Rayland suddenly finds himself in command of a short-handed Ranger post while also serving as the town’s temporary law. Texas Rangers were feared for their hard-handed treatment of outlaws, and Rayland brings that same iron resolve to the sheriff’s office. Though the wounded sheriff is reluctant to hand his town over to a Ranger sergeant, he has no other choice.

Meanwhile, Captain Moon rides west, ordered by his superiors to take most of his men to the border and end the violence there. When he arrives, he discovers that the leader of the Campbell gang has seized the sheriff’s office after the town’s lawman mysteriously disappears—later found buried in a shallow grave outside El Paso. The gang intends to take the small border town by force, install its own men in positions of power, and bleed the community dry. Merchants who refuse to pay are robbed or killed; the courthouse falls under outlaw control; and the mayor is taken hostage.

Then the situation turns from dangerous to desperate. The outlaw sheriff sends telegrams across Texas offering one-hundred-dollar bounties for the badges of sheriffs, deputies, and Texas Rangers—along with their revolvers as proof of death. Bounty hunters and opportunists alike rush to claim the reward, turning the state into a hunting ground for every man who wears a star. Mayhem follows, making Blood on the Saddle one of my most action-packed novels.

Ash Lingam
