Rafter O Ranch: The Complete Series

by Natalie Bright and Denise F. McAllister

Three heartfelt Christian Western romances follow the Olsen family through the challenges of ranch life. A marriage strains under an unexpected homecoming, an Army veteran clashes with a fire chief, and a singer must choose between stardom and the people she loves. Beneath the Texas sky, faith, family, and enduring love offer a way home.

Words from Natalie Bright

Welcome to the Rafter O Ranch, y’all!

Denise F. McAllister and I teamed up for another Western series which has all the vibes you love—small-town feels, underlying Texas flair and cowboy culture, clean, wholesome romance, and elements of faith.

For me, the inspiration behind this series was the one-stop-light farming community of Dimmitt, where I was known as “Peggy’s daughter.” Mom ran the cosmetic counter at the drugstore, and my father owned a welding shop. There was a time in my life when I couldn’t wait to leave, and now I find myself reflecting more and more about that community, the youth group at the First Baptist Church, and the bond that still remains among many of my hometown friends.

If you would have told “small-town me” that my future would include being the mother of two brilliant sons, author of books and owner of a cattle ranch, I would have thought you loco. The ranching truths in this series are based on real stories from our own ranch where we raise Angus beef on the flat, treeless grasslands of the Texas Panhandle. We still follow the culture and ranching traditions established over 150 years ago. Yes, the lifestyle is real.

Each book in the Rafter O Series can be read as a stand-alone or enjoy them in order. Look for me across all social platforms, and I am new to TikTok @natalie.bright3, so let’s connect!

Natalie Bright

Words from Denise F. McAllister

Natalie Bright and I enjoyed writing the Rafter O Ranch series. Whereas our first series, The Wild Cow Ranch, centered mainly on the life of Carli Jameson, Rafter O Ranch branched out to her neighbor down the road, Nathan Olsen.

In Home Is Where You Are, we wanted to write a different kind of cowboy story. Creating Indya, the young woman Nathan marries, was a lot of fun. She loves the art scene in Santa Fe, New Mexico. Her arc takes her from posh gallery openings to dusty Texas ranch roundups. How will she reconcile it all and still support Nathan?

Nathan’s father expected his oldest son to take over the ranch, but Nathan also had creativity coursing through his veins. He loved making art with his hands, even if, at first, he hid it from his parents.

One of my personal loves is Western art, so it was heartfelt to incorporate this into our story. Living in Georgia, I love visiting the Booth Western Art Museum in Cartersville. I could spend hours exploring and learning, always seeing something new that embraces my heart.

In Home Is Where You Are, one of Nathan’s big passions is sculpting. I am not a sculptor, but I so appreciate viewing the work. My research led me to learn about sculptor Chris Navarro from Sedona, Arizona.

We hope readers will enjoy escaping into these books. Spend time with the Olsen siblings and learn about their passions. Their lives have many wholesome and endearing moments, but twists and turns often cause drama and turbulence along the way.

Denise F. McAllister
