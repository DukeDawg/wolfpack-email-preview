Road Runner (Biker 7)

by Mike Baron

Josh Pratt rides into the Colorado Rockies to rescue a detective’s daughter from a deadly cult. With a fugitive hacker at his side and a crime boss closing in, the mission becomes a brutal fight for survival inside a fortified mountain compound.

Words from Mike Baron

Road Runner is the seventh Josh Pratt novel. Josh Pratt is a reformed motorcycle hoodlum who went to prison, found God, and got out determined to turn his life around. Madison police detective Heinz Calloway calls Josh when his daughter Ashley runs off with self-proclaimed New Age prophet Scipio to his camp in the Rockies. You know Scipio. Jim Jones. David Koresh. Aleister Crowley. Scipio calls his cult God's Breath.

Josh talks to terrified God's Breath survivors. God's Breath is ruthless in persecuting apostates. Josh gains a new techie, rapper/hacker Ninja Preston, who comes with his own problems. He's on the run from St. Louis gangster John Torreo, for outing Torreo's son as a woman abuser and drug dealer.

The God's Breath compound lies just below the Continental Divide. Attempting to reach the camp from the back side, Josh falls and is captured by Gladiator, Scipio's enforcer, and forced to fight for his life. Josh and Heinz save Ashley and bring her home but Scipio doesn't forgive or forget. Biker is noir for our age.
