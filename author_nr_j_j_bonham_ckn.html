A quick note

I didn’t start out to write this particular story. My interest was just to write a simple love story between an underprivileged young half-breed and an attractive young girl not of his station in life. But somewhere along the line the story began to write itself. I’m not kidding. What you start out to do when writing will not ever be what you exactly intended. Not when you’re writing from your soul. Take courage as it says in Second Timothy 1:7 God hath not given us the spirit of fear, but of power and love and a sound mind. And remember the more you outline and plan the less you allow something more than you to enter the story. When I was three, maybe four, I used to tell fantastic stories when I was asked what happened. My father would ask me, “Are you telling me a story? (as I’m a lie) Story telling is as old as we are. Sitting around a warming fire, men & women told their stories. We’re they TRUE? Who cares! There’s something called narrative truth which when encounter tweaks the heart and informs the soul. That’s the truth that Ariel’s Profit is all about. Every time I write a new book in that series I learn something about myself, and prayerfully I hope you open your heart and suspend your modern mind and find the kernels of narrative truth which just might change your life. Am I telling a story, you bet your bippy I am!
