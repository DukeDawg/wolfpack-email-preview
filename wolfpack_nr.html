Recovering Deputy U.S. Marshal Coble Bray investigates murders patterned after the Seven Deadly Sins and confronts Harlan Vexler, a calculating adversary who studies people before using their weaknesses against them.

Words from Darrel Sparkman

The Devil’s Due is #6 in the Coble Bray Western Mysteries. Research is one of the most enjoyable parts of writing this series, especially because the stories are set where I live and the history keeps surprising me. I planned to explore the Seven Deadly Sins, then realized I had already been doing it. Southwest Missouri was deadlier than famous places like Deadwood or the Lincoln County Wars—look into Joplin’s history and you’ll be amazed. There’s more Coble Bray to come.
